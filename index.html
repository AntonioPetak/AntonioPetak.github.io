Power BI Projects

Pregled realiziranih analitičkih rješenja i nadzornih ploča.

Insurance Operations & Executive Scorecard Dashboard

A multi-page Power BI executive scorecard designed for insurance management to monitor portfolio performance, premium trends, and claims servicing efficiency. Tracks top-level KPIs including Total Net Premium (€1.50M), Total Policies (1,500), Loss Ratio (30%), and Claims Severity (€5,000). Features deep-dive analytics for Claims Exposure (analyzing repair vs. replacement costs), Claims Servicing (SLA %, Resolution Times, and Aging analysis), and Premium Performance with dynamic forecasting and regional heatmaps. Includes side-panel slicers, KPI switchers, and drill-through table views.

Insurance Claims Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI reporting solution built for property and casualty insurance management to monitor, analyze, and optimize claim settlement workflows. The dashboard consolidates key financial metrics including Total Payable ($3.67M), Dwelling Coverage ($6.1M), Total Deductibles ($230.5K), and High-Value Claims (> $20k), while tracking operational efficiency from claim receipt through inspection. It delivers regional claims distribution across major urban centers in Croatia, categorizes financial losses by cause (Storm/Wind, Vehicle Impact, Water Damage, Fire, Vandalism), and evaluates adjusting firm performance. Includes granular line-item repair cost breakdowns, statement-of-loss statement analysis comparing ACV vs. RCV and Depreciation, interactive slide-out filter panels, custom anomaly detection, and root-cause analysis via Power BI Decomposition Trees.

Social Metrics Dashboard

A comprehensive multi-source Power BI marketing and social media analytics platform tracking performance across LinkedIn, CRM Lead Generation pipelines, and Google Analytics. Features real-time tracking of brand reach and audience engagement KPIs—including Impressions (146.16K), Reactions, Followers, and Post-level CTR/Click analytics—with demographic breakdowns by Industry (Consulting, IT Services, Finance) and geography. Integrates sales funnel analytics to monitor pipeline opportunities by both Volume (802 total, 32 won) and Revenue (€80.12M pipeline value, €2.93M won), tracking conversion velocity through each sales stage. Additionally, monitors Google Analytics web traffic (sessions, engagement duration, bounce rates), top-performing landing pages, and traffic acquisition sources (Direct, Paid, Social, Search, Email). Built with dynamic field parameter selectors, custom navigation bookmarks, and interactive Bing map visualizations.

Designer Clothing Budgeting Dashboard

A robust Power BI financial reporting and budgeting solution developed for luxury fashion retail management to control revenues, wholesale customer orders, and multi-pass budget revisions. Tracks high-level executive financial metrics comparing Actual Revenue ($14M) against initial ($20M) and second-pass ($22M) budget iterations, alongside client-level order distributions sorted from highest to lowest across major global department stores (e.g., Harrods, Galeries Lafayette, Saks Fifth Avenue, Dover Street Market). Features structured Budget vs. Actuals variance reports categorized by company groups and retail channels, stacked performance charts tracking actuals against budget passes over time, dynamic drill-downs using Decomposition Trees for regional sales breakdown analysis, and a custom slide-out filter panel for slicing data by season, gender, collection, and geographic territory.

Insurance Risk & Actuarial Analytics Dashboard

An advanced actuarial and underwriting analytics suite designed for property insurance risk profiling, loss exposure modeling, and spatial risk distribution. Provides flexible ad-hoc reporting tools allowing custom column selection to compute critical insurance metrics including Earned Premium, Incurred Losses, Earned House Years, Loss Ratio, Severity, Frequency, and Loss Cost per 1K Total Insured Value (TIV). Features an interactive risk factor breakdown powered by Power BI Decomposition Trees to analyze underlying cost drivers such as roof age, wiring age, HVAC type, and sprinkler systems. Incorporates spatial analytics using Bing Maps and high-resolution dark-mode ArcGIS geospatial mapping to visualize claim density and regional exposure across US regions, supported by dynamic KPI switchers and custom multi-category filter panels.
